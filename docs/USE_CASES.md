# Use cases

The engine is deployed wherever a person is about to ask you for something. Each case below shows the tools it
chose and why. Results are typical.

## Customer support

Ticket deflection is the industry's word for this. We just removed the steps in between.

> **Customer:** My order never arrived.
> **Engine:** Huh? `huh`
> **Customer:** I SAID MY ORDER NEVER ARRIVED.
> **Engine:** I'm sorry, let me take my AirPods out. What were you saying? `airpods`
> **Customer:** I paid ninety dollars for it.
> **Engine:** Damn, that's crazy. What's that got to do with me, though? `whats_that_got_to_do_with_me`
> **Customer:** I want a refund.
> **Engine:** I don't know about all of that. `i_dont_know_about_all_of_that`
> *Conflict resolved. Time to resolution: 41 seconds. Customer satisfaction: not collected.*
